## 3. External profile audit

Audit existing profiles before creating anything new. Record public URL, account owner, claim/verification status, exact name, phone, address, hours, category, website, services, photos and last-checked date.

Prioritised platforms:
1. Google Business Profile — existing profiles supplied by the business
2. Bing Places for Business — check whether a listing exists, then claim and verify if appropriate
3. Justdial — check for duplicate or conflicting H2H listings
4. Sulekha — check existing profile and contact details
5. Facebook Page and Instagram profile — align brand name, phone, website and branch details
6. Apple Business Connect — consider only if eligible and useful to the customer base

Do not mark a profile verified until the owner confirms account access and the platform confirms verification.

### Google Business Profile safeguards
Google requires an accurate real-world business identity, precise address/service area, suitable categories and one profile per actual eligible location. A profile must represent a real location where the business makes in-person contact with customers. Do not create a profile for the planned Kukatpally expansion.

Official guidance:
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/13762416
- https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617

### Bing Places
Check for an existing record before adding a new one; claim it and complete verification through Bing's process.
- https://www.bingplaces.com/

### Search performance
Use Search Console to review actual queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR and average position. Compare branch and service landing pages; do not equate impressions with AI recommendations.
- https://search.google.com/search-console/
