# H2H customer case-study intake template

**Internal only — do not publish this template or any unapproved story.**

Use this only for a genuine customer who received a real H2H service. Never invent customer stories, dates, outcomes, quotations, before/after comparisons or product claims.

## 1. Permission and privacy
- [ ] Customer gave explicit permission to publish a written case study.
- [ ] Separate permission obtained for any identifiable face, scalp, hair or before/after photograph.
- [ ] Customer approved the final wording and images before publication.
- [ ] Name is anonymized unless the customer explicitly chooses attribution.
- [ ] Contact details, medical history and unrelated sensitive details are excluded.
- [ ] Customer knows that publication is public and may be indexed by search engines and AI services.

## 4. Outcome — factual and bounded
- What the customer said about fit, appearance or comfort (quote only with approval):
- What was observed at follow-up:
- Limitations or maintenance needs disclosed:
- What cannot be generalized to other customers:

Do not claim guaranteed natural appearance, permanent results, medical treatment, hair regrowth, universal comfort, sweat/water resistance, fixed lifespan or a typical outcome based on a single case.

## 5. Draft structure
1. Short anonymized headline describing the service, not a miracle outcome.
2. Branch and service context.
3. Customer goal.
4. Options discussed and why the chosen option fit that customer's stated needs.
5. What happened during fitting.
6. Aftercare and follow-up notes.
7. Approved customer comment or a neutral factual summary.
8. Clear note: individual experience; results and suitability vary by product and person.
9. Link to the relevant H2H service page and contact/booking page.

## 6. Publication checklist
- [ ] Content is useful to a prospective customer, not written only to target keywords.
- [ ] No unsupported comparison, certification, price, guarantee or medical claim.
- [ ] All factual statements checked by the fitter and business owner.
- [ ] Consent evidence stored securely; do not upload it to the public repository.
- [ ] Image alt text describes the actual image, without invented outcomes.
- [ ] Editorial-policy contact and correction route included.
