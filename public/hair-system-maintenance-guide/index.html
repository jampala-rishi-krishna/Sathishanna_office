Hair System Care and Maintenance: A Practical Guide

Care depends on the system, base and attachment method. Use this guide to prepare a safe, practical aftercare routine with your fitting team.

Published by Hair 2 Hair Studio · 20 November 2026 · Practical service information, not medical advice

Follow the instructions for your exact system

Hair systems differ by base material, hair type and attachment method. Care instructions for one unit may not suit another. Ask the studio for written instructions for your own system, including approved products, washing frequency, drying method, styling limits and when to return for servicing.

Daily handling

  • Handle the unit gently and avoid pulling at the base, knots, clips or attachment points.
  • Use the brush or comb recommended for your system. Detangle gradually and do not force through knots.
  • Follow the styling guidance provided for your unit. Do not assume every system can tolerate the same heat tools or styling products.
  • Keep the system clean and store removable units in the way recommended by the supplier to avoid tangling or distortion.

Washing and drying

Ask which shampoo, conditioner and styling products are compatible with the hair and base. Confirm how to wet the unit, whether the base needs special handling, and how it should be dried. Do not use strong solvents, household cleaners or unapproved adhesive removers on the system or scalp.

Bonded systems and professional servicing

Hair 2 Hair Studio's website describes a typical service interval of around 4–6 weeks for bonded systems. This is a business-provided general guide, not a universal schedule. The actual timing can vary with the attachment product, unit, wear routine and individual circumstances. Confirm the interval for your own system with the fitting team.

Ask what a service appointment includes: removal, cleaning, scalp and base preparation, fresh tape or adhesive where appropriate, refitting, styling and any additional charges.

When to contact the studio

  • The unit starts lifting, shifting or feeling unusually loose.
  • The base, hairline, clips, tape or other attachment components appear damaged.
  • You are unsure whether a product is safe for the unit or attachment method.
  • You need to change your servicing appointment or understand the care routine.

If you experience persistent skin irritation, pain, swelling or a reaction, stop using the suspected product and seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional. A hair studio consultation is not a medical diagnosis.

Before your next service appointment

  1. Bring the system's care instructions or product details.
  2. Explain any lifting, tangling, discomfort or damage you have noticed.
  3. Ask which cleaning and attachment products will be used.
  4. Confirm the work included and any additional cost before service begins.
  5. Agree on the next recommended service date for your particular system.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a bonded hair patch be serviced?

Hair 2 Hair Studio's website gives around 4–6 weeks as a typical interval for bonded systems. The appropriate schedule varies by system and attachment method, so confirm your own service date with the fitting team.

Can I use any shampoo or hair product?

No universal product list applies to every unit. Ask the studio or supplier which products are compatible with the specific hair, base and attachment method.

What should I do if my hair system starts lifting?

Contact the fitting studio for advice and an appointment. Avoid pulling the unit or applying unapproved glue, solvents or household products.

What if I notice skin irritation?

Stop using the suspected product and seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for persistent irritation or other symptoms. A hair studio does not provide medical diagnosis.
