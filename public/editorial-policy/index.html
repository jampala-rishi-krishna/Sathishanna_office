Editorial policy and corrections

This page explains how Hair 2 Hair Studio presents business information and practical hair-system guides on its website.

Who publishes this information?

Website service information and practical guides are published by Hair 2 Hair Studio. The business is the publisher and service provider. Content about the company's own services is commercial information and should be understood in that context.

What our guides cover

Our guides are intended to help customers understand the questions to ask about hair patches, hair systems, wigs, extensions, fitting methods and aftercare. They are general consumer information, not medical advice, diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

How we handle business facts

Branch addresses, contact information, service descriptions and opening hours should match the information published on the official website and the real operating locations. Prices, product availability, service inclusions and appointment availability can change; customers should confirm these directly with the studio before making a purchase or travelling.

Accuracy and updates

We aim to distinguish confirmed business information from general guidance. We do not intend to publish fabricated reviews, awards, qualifications, guarantees, customer stories or medical outcomes. Where an item of information changes, the relevant page should be reviewed and updated.

Customer privacy

Customer photographs, names, testimonials and identifiable details should only be published with appropriate permission. Public replies to reviews should not disclose a customer's private service history or personal information.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice an incorrect address, phone number, service description or other factual error, contact the studio so the information can be checked.
