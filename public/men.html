Men's hair patches and hair systems

Hair 2 Hair Studio offers consultations for men considering a hair patch or non-surgical hair system. Start by discussing the area you want covered, your preferred hairline and density, day-to-day routine, fitting method and maintenance expectations.

Choose a base that suits your needs

Skin or polyurethane (PU), lace, monofilament and hybrid constructions are different ways a hair system may be built. Each has trade-offs in appearance, airflow, handling and maintenance. The exact construction varies by product, so ask to inspect the specific base before choosing.

Plan the fit and finish

Discuss the coverage area, hairline design, hair direction, colour match and density. A good consultation should explain what can realistically be matched, how the system is attached and what care it requires.

Understand attachment and aftercare

Removable and bonded systems have different daily-care and servicing needs. Ask about removal, cleaning, re-fixing, adhesive suitability and the recommended service interval for the exact system being considered.

Frequently asked questions

Is a hair patch a surgical procedure?

A hair patch or hair system is a non-surgical hair replacement option. It is different from a hair transplant. Discuss suitability and any scalp concerns with an appropriately qualified professional.

Which base is best: skin, lace, mono or hybrid?

There is no single best base for everyone. Compare the exact construction, comfort, intended use, attachment method and maintenance before deciding.

Can I get a colour and density match?

Ask the fitting team to assess your existing hair and show available colour and density options. The final match depends on the product and your own hair.
