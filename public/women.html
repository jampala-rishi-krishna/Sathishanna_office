Hair toppers and patches

A topper or patch may be considered when coverage is needed in a particular area, such as the parting or crown. Ask about the base construction, attachment method, coverage area, colour and density match.

Full wigs

A full wig provides coverage over the whole scalp. Before choosing, confirm the hair fibre, cap construction, size, fit, styling limitations, maintenance and whether customisation is available for the selected product.

Hair extensions

Extensions can add length or volume when suitable for the wearer's existing hair. Ask how the chosen method is attached, how it affects your natural hair, and what maintenance or removal is required.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose a topper or a full wig?

A topper is generally intended for partial coverage, while a full wig covers the scalp. The suitable option depends on your coverage needs, comfort and preferred styling.

Are human hair options available?

Hair 2 Hair Studio lists human hair wigs among its services. Confirm the available fibre, construction, colour and current stock for the specific item before ordering.

Can a topper be matched to my existing hair?

Ask for an in-person or otherwise suitable assessment of colour, texture, density and coverage. The match depends on the available product and your natural hair.
