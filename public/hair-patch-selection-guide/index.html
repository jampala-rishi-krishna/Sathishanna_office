How to Choose a Hair Patch or Hair System: A Practical Guide

A practical consultation checklist for comparing hair patch bases, hair quality, attachment options and the full cost of ownership.

Published by Hair 2 Hair Studio · 20 November 2026 · Practical service information, not medical advice

Start with the result you need

Before choosing a hair patch or hair system, identify the area you want covered, the look you prefer, and how you expect to wear and maintain the system. A small crown area, a receding hairline and fuller coverage can require different designs.

Hair 2 Hair Studio lists skin, lace, mono and hybrid base systems for men, along with patches, toppers and wigs for women. The exact options available should be confirmed with the studio at the time of consultation.

Understand the base options

Skin base

A skin-style base uses a thin film construction. Ask to see the actual thickness, finish, attachment method and cleaning instructions for the specific unit. “Skin base” can describe different products, so compare samples rather than relying on the name alone.

Lace base

A lace base uses a mesh construction. Ask how the hairline is finished, how the base should be handled, which attachment methods are supported and what routine care it needs.

Mono base

A monofilament base uses a fine mesh-like foundation. Ask the consultant to show the actual construction, how the hair is ventilated into the base and how the unit is maintained.

Hybrid base

A hybrid system combines more than one base material or construction. Ask which sections use each material and why that combination is being suggested for your coverage and styling needs.

There is no single base that is best for everyone. The suitability depends on the specific product, design, fitting method, wear routine and the customer's preferences.

Compare the hair and the finish

  • Colour: compare the sample in natural light with your existing hair, including greys and highlights.
  • Texture: check whether the wave or curl pattern blends with your own hair.
  • Density: ask to see the system styled at a density that looks consistent with the rest of your hair.
  • Length and haircut: agree how much cutting or blending is included in the fitting appointment.
  • Hairline: view the front edge from normal conversation distance and ask how it is maintained.

Ask how the system will be attached

Attachment methods may include adhesive or tape bonding, weaving, or clips, depending on the system and the customer's needs. These methods are not interchangeable for every base. Ask which method is compatible with the specific system, what removal involves and how often it needs professional servicing.

Get an itemised quote before deciding

Request a written quote that separates the hair system, customisation, fitting, haircut or styling, adhesives or tapes, aftercare products and future servicing. Ask whether any follow-up adjustment is included and what would incur an additional charge. Prices and inclusions can change, so confirm the current amount directly with the studio.

Consultation checklist

  1. Which base construction is being recommended, and can I inspect a physical sample?
  2. What hair type, density, colour and length are included?
  3. Which attachment method is compatible with this unit?
  4. How is the unit removed, cleaned and re-fitted?
  5. What are the home-care instructions and recommended service interval?
  6. What exactly is included in the quote, and what is charged separately?
  7. What should I do if the fit feels uncomfortable or the base is damaged?

Frequently asked questions

Which hair patch base is best?

There is no single best base for everyone. Compare actual skin, lace, mono or hybrid samples, attachment compatibility, handling and maintenance requirements before deciding.

What should I compare before buying a hair system?

Compare base construction, hair colour, texture, density, length, hairline finish, attachment method, aftercare and the full cost including future servicing.

Can I choose the attachment method myself?

The available attachment method depends on the system and base. Ask which methods are compatible and how removal and servicing are performed.
