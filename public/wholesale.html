Wholesale hair products for salons and trade buyers

Hair 2 Hair Studio invites enquiries from salons, retailers and other trade customers interested in wholesale hair products. Share the product category, intended use, quantity and delivery location so the team can confirm current availability and terms.

Product categories to enquire about

Trade customers can ask about men's hair systems, women's patches and toppers, wigs, extension products and related accessories. Product availability, specifications and order options should be confirmed directly.

Information to include in an enquiry

To receive a useful response, include your business name, location, product category, approximate quantity, preferred specifications and whether you need repeat supply or a one-time order.

Confirm terms before placing an order

Request the current catalogue, product specifications, sample policy, minimum order quantity (if any), pricing, payment terms, dispatch arrangements, delivery coverage and return or defect process in writing. Do not rely on assumptions about stock or delivery timelines.

Frequently asked questions

Who can make a wholesale enquiry?

Salons, retailers, distributors and other trade buyers can contact Hair 2 Hair Studio to discuss their requirements.

Can I get a product catalogue and wholesale pricing?

Contact the team with your product requirements and ask for the current catalogue, availability and written quotation.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities, if applicable, depend on the product and current trade terms. Confirm them directly before placing an order.

Does the business ship to my location?

Share your delivery location and order details so the team can confirm whether delivery is currently available and provide the applicable terms.
