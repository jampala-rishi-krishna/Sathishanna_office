Hair bonding, weaving and extension services

Hair 2 Hair Studio lists hair bonding, weaving, extensions and related hair system services. Fitting methods differ in how they attach to the hair, how they are maintained and how they are removed. Discuss your existing hair, scalp comfort, routine and expectations before selecting a method.

Hair bonding

Bonding attaches a hair system or patch using an adhesive selected for the fitting. Ask what adhesive is proposed, how the scalp and base are prepared, how removal is handled and what servicing interval applies to that particular system.

Hair weaving

Weaving uses a thread-based attachment approach rather than the same adhesive process used in bonding. Ask how the weave is fitted, how tension is managed, how it is cleaned and how it is safely removed or adjusted.

Clip-in solutions and extensions

Clip-in options are designed to be attached and removed by the wearer. Other extension methods may be semi-permanent. Confirm the exact technique, expected wear, removal process and care instructions before proceeding.

Maintenance and follow-up

Care routines vary by base, attachment method, activity and individual needs. Follow the instructions for the exact system and confirm when to return for cleaning, adjustment or re-fixing.

Frequently asked questions

Is bonding the same as weaving?

No. Bonding uses an adhesive attachment method; weaving uses a thread-based method. Their fitting, removal and care requirements differ.

How long does a bonded hair system last before servicing?

There is no universal interval. It depends on the exact system, adhesive, scalp, activity and care routine. Ask the fitter for instructions specific to your system.

Are clip-in options removable?

Clip-in solutions are intended to be attached and removed, but follow the product's fitting and care instructions to avoid unnecessary pulling or damage.

Can I enquire about maintenance?

Yes. Contact Hair 2 Hair Studio to ask about the care and follow-up services available for the system you use or are considering.
